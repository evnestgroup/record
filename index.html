How to check it yourself

Each pick's fingerprint is the SHA-256 of the text shown when you open it. That text starts with the previous pick's fingerprint, so changing, removing or back-dating any pick breaks every fingerprint after it. Open a pick to see its text, or download record.json and hash it with any tool.

Sealed picks are for games that haven't started. Only their fingerprint is shown until kickoff. It's published before the game, so it couldn't have been made after.

Results and closing line value are added after each game and aren't part of the fingerprint. They come from the final score and Pinnacle's last price before the game.
